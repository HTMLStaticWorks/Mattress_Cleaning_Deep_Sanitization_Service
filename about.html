OUR STORY & PHILOSOPHY

ELEVATING HOME HYGIENE TO AN EDITORIAL STANDARD

PureSanity was founded on a simple principle: every home deserves deep, clinical fabric care delivered with white-glove hospitality.

THE FOUNDING VISION

REDEFINING MATTRESS & UPHOLSTERY SANITIZATION

Traditional home cleaning services often relied on superficial vacuuming or wet scrubbing that left fabrics damp for days. PureSanity introduced mobile high-pressure micro-extraction and fabric-safe UV technology to the residential space.

Today, our mobile units serve over 10,000 residences, boutique hotels, and rental properties with standardized technician training and transparent package rates.

COMMERCIAL-GRADE EQUIPMENT

THE TOOLS BEHIND THE SANITIZATION

We invest exclusively in heavy-duty mobile extraction units and certified UV light systems.

Dual Motor Extraction

Commercial vacuum motors providing superior lift velocity for fast drying times.

UV-C Sanitization Lamps

Calibrated light passes targeting micro-contaminants on fabric top layers.

pH-Balanced Detergents

Biodegradable, non-toxic cleaning solutions safe for children and household pets.
